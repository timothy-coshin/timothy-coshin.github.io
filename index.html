About

I am an AI researcher specializing in graph and language models. I hold a Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from Yonsei University, where my research focused on integrating graph-structured knowledge with large language models. I also hold a Bachelor’s degree in Theology and Computer Science from Yonsei University, and have pursued additional studies in Theology at Sungkonghoe University and in English Language and Literature at Korea National Open University. My research interests include graph-augmented large language models, neuro-symbolic reasoning, and the use of AI for sign language translation and generation.
